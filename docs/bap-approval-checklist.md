# BAP approval checklist

Use this checklist with the coach/promotor before committing to a subject.

## 1. Shared questions

- [ ] Who is the primary stakeholder?
- [ ] What decision or research question must the MVP support?
- [ ] Is the subject individual, duo or group work?
- [ ] Which datasets and permissions are approved for the analysis semester?
- [ ] What evidence is sufficient for a passing MVP?
- [ ] Which stretch goals are explicitly excluded from the MVP?

## 2. Three Ages — go/no-go

Proceed only if all three are answered positively:

- [ ] A permitted historical-image source with at least two usable epochs is identified.
- [ ] An official source for register construction years is identified.
- [ ] A reviewer accepts manual, uncertain labels instead of requiring city-wide automation.

**Proposed MVP:** six to ten source-linked Brussels buildings, two image epochs, a documented annotation protocol, and an explainable explorer.

## 3. Trees & Surfaces — go/no-go

Proceed only if all three are answered positively:

- [ ] A stakeholder accepts a descriptive heat/mobility analysis as useful.
- [ ] A mobility proxy stronger or more defensible than nearest-counter distance is available, or the limitation is explicitly accepted.
- [ ] One heat metric and one bounded intervention/descriptive question are agreed.

**Proposed MVP:** one city, one heat measure, one labelled mobility proxy, transparent normalization and a limitations report.
