## Existing brainstorming

The brainstorming explored several directions, including energy, public transport, municipal budgets, finance, fantasy-sports forecasting, data quality, agent evaluation, buildings and housing.

The imported FPP notes record **De Vier Prijzen van Brussel** as the selected FPP direction:

> Compare the market asking price, legal reference rent, fiscal value and real transaction price for Brussels housing, and make their divergence explainable and searchable by neighbourhood.

The current product concept is a data product with:

1. a unified property record;
2. independent data-regime plug-ins;
3. divergence analysis and uncertainty;
4. an interactive explorer and methodological report.

The FPP notes also describe a hard fallback: start with the open legal reference-rent regime and public/approved market or transaction data. Fiscal or restricted data must never be a prerequisite.
